What Is Included in a Mortgage Payment?

A typical mortgage payment consists of four components, often called PITI: Principal (the amount borrowed), Interest (the cost of borrowing), Taxes (property taxes), and Insurance (homeowner's insurance). Some payments also include HOA (Homeowners Association) fees.

2026 Mortgage Rates

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate in 2026 is approximately 6.5%–7.0%. A 15-year fixed mortgage typically offers a lower rate, around 5.8%–6.3%, but requires higher monthly payments. Rates vary based on your credit score, down payment, loan type, and lender.

How Much House Can I Afford?

A common rule of thumb is that your monthly mortgage payment should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income. For example, if you earn $6,000 per month, your target mortgage payment would be around $1,680 or less. Lenders also look at your total debt-to-income ratio, which should ideally stay below 43%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good down payment for a house?
A 20% down payment is traditionally recommended because it avoids Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI). However, many loan programs allow down payments as low as 3%–5% for first-time buyers. FHA loans require just 3.5% down.
How much is a monthly payment on a $300,000 house?
With a 20% down payment ($60,000), a $240,000 loan at 6.8% for 30 years results in a principal and interest payment of approximately $1,568/month. Adding taxes and insurance typically brings the total to $1,900–$2,200/month depending on your location.
Is it better to get a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?
A 15-year mortgage has higher monthly payments but saves significantly on total interest paid — often $100,000 or more over the life of the loan. A 30-year mortgage offers lower payments and more flexibility. The best choice depends on your financial goals and monthly budget.
What credit score do I need to buy a house?
For a conventional mortgage, most lenders require a minimum credit score of 620. For the best interest rates, aim for 740 or higher. FHA loans accept scores as low as 580 with a 3.5% down payment.
How much are property taxes on a house?
Property tax rates vary by state and county. The national average is about 1.1% of the home's assessed value per year. States like New Jersey and Illinois have rates above 2%, while Alabama and Hawaii are below 0.5%.
